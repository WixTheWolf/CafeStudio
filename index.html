    The medium

    Painted with coffee.

    I use instant coffee diluted with water to create a versatile painting medium. It offers a range of transparency similar to watercolor, yet it can be built up in layers to achieve the opacity of acrylics. I also love layering the medium to create a subtle sculptural effect adding texture and dramatic depth to my work.
